# Concept Formalization Protocol

Use this template for every concept entering the framework.

## 1. Author's intended meaning
Preserve the author's wording or a clearly marked paraphrase.

## 2. Ordinary and disciplinary meanings
Record relevant meanings from mathematics, logic, physics, computer science, psychology, philosophy, or another domain. Identify the discipline and source when external facts are involved.

## 3. Scope
Specify what the concept applies to and what it does not claim to apply to.

## 4. Type
Is it an entity, property, relation, operation, process, representation, proposition, value, or evaluation rule? More than one type may be required, but justify that choice.

## 5. Formal definition
Give a set-theoretic, logical, algebraic, graphical, probabilistic, computational, or other appropriate definition. Do not force an equation if another formal representation is clearer.

## 6. Inputs and outputs
Specify what the concept operates on and what result it yields.

## 7. Assumptions and axioms
List each assumption explicitly. Mark axioms as adopted assumptions, not discovered facts.

## 8. Examples and non-examples
Include cases where the definition clearly applies and cases where it does not.

## 9. Cross-domain realizations
Show how the structure is instantiated in different fields. Do not assume the meanings or governing laws are identical across domains.

## 10. Consequences and predictions
Derive what follows from the definition or model. Separate logical consequences from empirical predictions.

## 11. Counterexamples and failure conditions
State what would show that the definition is inadequate, the hypothesis false, or the model limited in scope.

## 12. Dependencies
Record which concepts this one depends upon and which might be derivable from it. Check for circularity.

## 13. Confidence and status
Choose: AUTHOR IDEA, DEFINITION, AXIOM, DERIVATION, ESTABLISHED RESULT, HYPOTHESIS, COUNTEREXAMPLE, or OPEN QUESTION.

## 14. Revision history
Record what changed, why it changed, and which older version it supersedes.
