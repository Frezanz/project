# Original Ideas — Working Record

This file preserves the author's ideas as distinct from later formalizations. These are paraphrases of the discussion, not claims that the wording is a verbatim quotation.

## Core proposals
1. **Relations as a common foundation:** connection, relation, and measurement are the same underlying phenomenon in different forms.
2. **Measurement in a broad sense:** everything may be measurable in some way because measuring is relating, but not everything can be measured by the same method.
3. **Cross-domain applicability:** the concepts should be applicable, with appropriate interpretation, across physics, human psychology, philosophy, logic, conscience, and morality.
4. **Perspective matters:** a word's meaning may differ between people; therefore, a claim must be evaluated according to what it specifically means and describes, not merely according to an alternate interpretation.
5. **Claim versus described situation:** a sentence can exist as an expression even when the situation it asserts is false. The existence of the sentence must not be confused with the truth of its content.
6. **Truth and stability:** the author proposes investigating whether truth or the strength of a truth can be characterized through stability under change. This requires precise definitions and may need refinement; stability and truth should not be assumed identical.
7. **Physical and nonphysical or imagined concepts:** the framework should distinguish physical things, imagined entities, abstract objects, representations, and experiences while investigating relations between them.
8. **No requirement for a second physical object:** the absence of an external comparison object does not by itself settle whether internal distinctions or self-relations are possible; this needs formal treatment.

## Guardrails
- Do not rewrite these ideas into stronger claims without marking the change.
- Do not treat an analogy as a proof.
- Do not say all claims are factually true merely because their expressions exist. Ask which proposition and interpretation are being evaluated.
- Do not assume every relation is a conventional measurement. Clarify what the broader use of *measurement* means and what it enables us to distinguish.
- Preserve disagreements and revisions in the changelog rather than erasing the original thought.
