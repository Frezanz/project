# Relational Framework

An evolving research project exploring whether connection, relation, measurement, perspective, truth, change, meaning, and other concepts can be understood through a common formal structure.

## Aim
Develop a framework that is as precise, coherent, and internally consistent as possible, and test whether it can explain useful structures across physics, logic, mathematics, psychology, philosophy, and ethics.

**Status:** exploratory hypothesis, not an established unified theory. Completeness, consistency, and empirical adequacy must be demonstrated, not assumed.

## Working principles
1. Preserve the author's original ideas separately from editorial interpretations and established knowledge.
2. Define terms before deriving conclusions from them.
3. Distinguish entities, concepts representing entities, propositions, and the states those propositions describe.
4. Treat proposed unifications as hypotheses to formalize and test.
5. Record assumptions, scope, counterexamples, and unresolved questions.
6. Prefer explicit relations and definitions over vague analogies.
7. Revise the framework when contradictions, counterexamples, or better models are found.

## Starting hypothesis
**Author's idea:** Connections, relations, and measurement may be different forms of a common underlying structure; measurement is relational rather than limited to physical quantities such as length or mass.

This is a hypothesis to clarify, not yet a formal axiom. We must determine whether connection, relation, and measurement are identical, related but distinct, or derivable from a more primitive concept.

## Status labels
- **AUTHOR IDEA:** what the author has proposed
- **DEFINITION:** a stipulated precise meaning
- **AXIOM:** an assumption adopted within a formal system
- **DERIVATION:** a conclusion logically derived from specified assumptions
- **ESTABLISHED RESULT:** a result supported within a named discipline or source
- **HYPOTHESIS:** a claim requiring investigation
- **COUNTEREXAMPLE:** a case that challenges a claim
- **OPEN QUESTION:** unresolved

## First milestone
Define *entity*, *distinction*, *relation*, *connection*, and *measurement* precisely enough to test whether some can be reduced to others, without building circular definitions.
